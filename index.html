About Arrant HR Solutions

Our Journey

We are an innovative full-service Human Resources consultancy offering a broad spectrum of professional services. Established in the year March 2008, "ARRANT HR Solutions" has emerged as a professionally managed and renowned HR recruitment consultancy firm in Tamil Nadu. A team of HR professionals from reputed management institutes mans the company with expertise in the area of Career Counseling, Recruitment (National & International), Training and Development, Eventz, and Digital Marketing.

In a short span of time, we understand our client's business and had been successfully delivering to their ongoing demands, in IT/ITES, Engineering, Construction, Mechanical, Pharmaceutical, Educational Institutions, Hospital, FMCG, Banking & Finance, Healthcare, etc. domains, taking time and care to understand client's strategies and goals as well as their recruitment needs. We are specialized in talent acquisitions & executive search & have expertise in headhunting the right talent for the right job.

We also encompass a database of potential and result-oriented candidates across India. We aim to provide personalized attention on shortlisting, collecting, and maintaining the best data bank based on market demand as well as strong linkages with academia and workplace professionalism. ARRANT HR Solutions has extended its footing into big corporate houses and other industries. We stand as a national and international supplier with a wholly-owned company structure. This organizational structure facilitates centralized decision-making and ensures consistent implementation of all corporate directives and customer-specific service delivery. It also enables us to provide our vendors with the most responsive and focused service possible.

Our endeavor is to recruit high-caliber professionals across all functional areas thereby maintaining a very strong and premium data bank of candidates. Our rigorous screening procedure ensures quality output within specific timeframes. In fact, our clients can rely on us to handle even the most critical requirements in the shortest possible time, which is of paramount importance in these evolving times.

ARRANT HR Solutions is committed to building talent within your organization, allowing you to extend sustainable service excellence to your Clients. We also recognize that attracting, selecting, and managing this pool of caliber candidates is a key factor in achieving this and many other critical goals within your organization and have therefore developed a customized solution that we feel will support this aim. Our aim is to delight our clients with our efficient services as well as put them at ease with their recruiting hassles.
